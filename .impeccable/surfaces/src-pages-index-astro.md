# Home page

Scope: the home page (`src/pages/index.astro`). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Calgary students, parents arriving on phones from WeChat or a teacher's email, teachers, sponsors and would-be student organizers. They need to know what ARC is, what is next and how to take part.

Action: get on the newsletter for the next event's registration (or register when a form is open). Proof: real event photos, a real released contest problem, the list of what has actually run.

Constraints set by the owner: keep the richer layout and motion; white pages with teal as the only accent and pale teal tints. No dark or saturated colour bands, no amber, no storytelling metaphor. No invented statistics, testimonials or sponsor logos. ARCane is not shown. Content stays visible without JavaScript and under reduced motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A lively youth-nonprofit home page whose liveliness comes from layout, real photos, large type and motion, not colour. It refuses dark bands, a second accent and themed concepts.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, near-black ink (#0e1f1d), one teal (#0b7566) for buttons, links and the date tile; pale teal (#e9f6f2) for the opening and sign-up bands; pale grey-teal (#f3f7f6) for alternating bands; white cards with 24px corners, hairline insets and soft layered shadows; pill buttons with a darker lower edge; wash pills for short facts; Bricolage Grotesque headings over Figtree text.

STORY: The visitor learns ARC runs free STEM contests and classes, by students, in Calgary; sees the next event and how to hear when registration opens; sees what ARC runs; tries a real problem; sees a contest day; learns who pays and who runs it; reads parent answers; signs up for dates.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Pale teal band shared with the header. Left: large ink headline with "Free" in teal, a two-sentence intro, a teal button and an outline one. Right: three overlapping event photos, two slightly rotated. Spanning the width and hanging over the band's bottom edge: a white next-event card with a teal date tile, facts and the sign-up button. On phones: headline, intro, card, photos.

FORM: The category standard, pinned by the owner in plain words. Seed key 1f029a03.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
