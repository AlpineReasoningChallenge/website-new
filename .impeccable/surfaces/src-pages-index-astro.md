# Home page

Scope: the home page (`src/pages/index.astro`) and its 3D scene (`src/scene/arc-scene.js`). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Calgary students, parents arriving on phones from WeChat or a teacher's email, teachers, sponsors and would-be student organizers. They need to know what ARC is, what is next and how to take part.

Owner's brief for this version (2026-11-12): the previous build looked "visibly vibecoded", "amateur", "toyish". Make it more professional and less cluttered, with a palette that fits ARC's logo, smoother scrolling and a 3D morphing side animation in the spirit of Math Attack, without applying animation to everything.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, professional page on one frost ground whose single moving idea is ARC's own logo built in 3D: the peaks, star and arc float beside the headline, then fly down the side and lie down as the sample problem's rhythm. It refuses card grids, badge rows, coloured bands and animation on everything.

OWN-WORLD: Frost ground #eef3f3 across the whole page; ink #0c1a1f taken from the logo's night sky; one teal #0b7566 for actions and the date; the logo's teal #52d5bf only in the 3D scene and on the night footer; white panels with hairline insets and soft ink-tinted shadows; Mona Sans throughout, slightly wide and tightly tracked for headings; hairline-ruled rows; sharp prism peaks with one teal face and teal edges.

STORY: The visitor learns ARC runs free STEM contests and classes, by students, in Calgary; sees the next event and how to hear when registration opens; scans what ARC runs; tries a real problem while the 3D notes play its rhythm; sees a contest day; learns who runs it and who pays; reads parent answers; signs up.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating frosted header bar. Left column: a large ink headline over three lines, a two-sentence intro, a teal button and a white one, then a white next-event panel (large teal day number, the event name as a link, three facts, the sign-up button). Right column: the 3D logo (five prism peaks, star, arc), its arc level with the headline, rising on load and tilting with the pointer. The panel sits in the left column so the right column is one clear lane for the logo's flight down to the rhythm stage. On phones: headline, intro, panel, then a still render of the 3D logo.

FORM: The category standard, pinned by the owner, with Math Attack's scroll-driven 3D staging as the signature. Seed key 1f029a03.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
